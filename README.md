## Research

### LiDAR for an Autonomous Model Car
**Bachelor's thesis · In progress · University of Debrecen**  
**Supervisor:** Dr. József Sütő

*Integration and Evaluation of LiDAR Sensors for an Autonomous Model Car*

My thesis investigates how LiDAR measurements can support environmental perception, obstacle detection, and autonomous navigation. The work aims to integrate a LiDAR sensor into a model car and develop software to acquire, process, and interpret its measurements.

Planned experiments will evaluate the benefits and limitations of the system, focusing on **detection accuracy, response time, and navigation reliability**.

## Selected Projects

### [Intelligent Course Scheduler](https://github.com/yamenshelbayeh/intelligent-course-scheduler)
Academic planning with **A\* search** and **constraint satisfaction**. Compared search and scheduling strategies through benchmarks, with 66 automated tests and a FastAPI/PostgreSQL/Streamlit application.

### [Traffic Sign Recognition](https://github.com/yamenshelbayeh/traffic-sign-recognition)
A TensorFlow/Keras CNN for **43 GTSRB traffic-sign classes**, achieving **98.71% test accuracy** on a stratified 60/40 train/test split. Developed through CS50 AI, with experiments in image preprocessing and network architecture.

### [BERT Attention & Masked Token Visualizer](https://github.com/yamenshelbayeh/bert-attention-visualization)
Exploration of a pretrained BERT model using Hugging Face Transformers: masked-token prediction and attention visualization across **12 layers and 12 heads**. Developed through CS50 AI.
